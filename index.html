Welcome

A coffee shop built around people, not rush.

Maple & Mortar started with a simple idea: great coffee tastes better when you have time to enjoy it. Every morning we pull fresh shots, steam milk by hand, and slide warm pastries out of the oven so your first stop of the day feels like a real one.

Pull up a chair, open a book, or just watch the steam rise. We'll keep the pot warm.
