Our story

Built by hand, one cup at a time

Maple & Mortar grew out of a tiny pop-up cart and a stubborn belief that a neighborhood deserves a place to sit down.

How it started

From a sidewalk cart to a corner of the block

In 2019, co-founders Mara and Theo parked a hand-painted cart on the corner of Elm and 3rd and started pulling shots for anyone who stopped. The line grew, the regulars stuck around, and two years later they found the brick storefront across the street — the one with the big windows and the creaky hardwood floor.

They kept the name, kept the cart for weekend events, and built out the bakery the neighborhood kept asking for. Today the same two people still open the shop most mornings, and the espresso machine still hisses before sunrise.

What we care about

Our values

01

Sourced with care

We buy from small farms that pay fair wages and farm sustainably. Every origin is traceable back to the people who grew it.

02

Made by hand

No automated push-button machines. Our baristas dial in every shot, steam every pitcher, and pour every drink to order.

03

A room for everyone

WiFi, power outlets, big tables, quiet corners. We built a space where you can stay a while — students, remote workers, and weekend regulars alike.
